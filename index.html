Scam Alert helps you check a phone number, text message, or link before you reply, tap, or send money. It is a community reporting tool for suspected scams. It is not a bank, a phone company, or a law-enforcement service, and a result is not a guarantee that a number or link is safe. You can look up a phone number and see whether it has been reported as a suspected scam. You can paste a text message or a link and compare it with reports already in the app. If something looks wrong, you can submit your own report with the number, message, or link, a category, and a short note. Other people can then see that report when they check the same number, message, or link. The app shows alerts for items that match community reports, so you can decide whether to answer a call, open a text, or follow a link. You can browse recent reports and filter them by type, such as phone calls, text messages, or links. Reports that are abusive, clearly wrong, or unlawful can be reviewed and removed. If you allow the related Android permission, the app can check a number or message on your device against known reports. You can use the lookup and reporting features without granting SMS, call log, or contacts access. The app does not upload your full contact list, call history, or inbox. Scam Alert does not ask you to create an account to check a report or submit one. It does not sell your personal information. Questions about a report or a data request can be sent to joshuadavidson719@gmail.com. Before you trust a result, remember that new scams may not be reported yet, and a report can be mistaken. Do not share passwords, one-time codes, or payment details with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly. If you think you have lost money, contact your bank and local authorities.
